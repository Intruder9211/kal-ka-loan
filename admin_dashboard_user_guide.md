# Money Viora: Admin Dashboard Guide

Welcome to the **Money Viora Admin Dashboard**! This is your central control room where you can manage your entire loan business. We have designed this to be as straightforward as possible. 

Here is a simple breakdown of what every section does and how you should use it.

---

### 📊 1. Dashboard (Overview)
**What it is:** The first screen you see when you log in. Think of it as the "front page of your newspaper."
**What you do here:** 
- Get a quick snapshot of how your business is doing today.
- See how many new applications have arrived, how many active loans you have, and your total outstanding amounts.
- See recent notifications and alerts at a glance.

### 👥 2. Customers
**What it is:** Your digital phonebook of everyone who has created an account on your website.
**What you do here:** 
- When someone signs up on your website, they instantly appear here—even if they haven't applied for a loan yet.
- Click on any customer to see their contact details, how long they have been registered, and a summary of their activity.

### 📝 3. Applications
**What it is:** Your inbox for new business. When a customer actually requests a loan, it lands here.
**What you do here:** 
- Review incoming loan requests.
- Move applications through different stages using the **Status** button (e.g., change it from "Submitted" ➡️ "Under Review" ➡️ "Documents Required" ➡️ "Approved").
- You can also add private "Internal Notes" on an application that only your team can see (e.g., "Called customer, waiting for salary slip").

### 📁 4. Documents
**What it is:** The filing cabinet where all customer uploads are safely stored.
**What you do here:** 
- When you ask a customer for their PAN Card, Aadhar, or Income Proof, their uploads appear here.
- You can view the files, download them, and mark them as "Verified" or "Rejected" (if the image is blurry, for example).

### 💰 5. Loans & Payments
**What it is:** The management area for applications that have already been *approved* and money has been given out.
**What you do here:** 
- **Loans:** Track active loans, see how much is outstanding, and monitor the tenure (timeline).
- **Payments:** Record and track EMIs paid by the customers. If someone pays, you update it here so their balance goes down.

### 🛍️ 6. Loan Products
**What it is:** Your store catalog.
**What you do here:** 
- Create or edit the types of loans you offer (e.g., "Home Loan", "Personal Loan").
- Set the rules for these loans, like the minimum/maximum amount they can ask for, and the standard interest rates.

### 🏦 7. Lenders
**What it is:** The directory of banks or financial institutions you partner with to provide these loans.
**What you do here:** 
- Add partner banks, their logos, and the specific rules or interest rates they offer.

### ✍️ 8. Website Content (Articles & FAQs)
**What it is:** The control panel for what regular visitors see on your public website.
**What you do here:** 
- **Articles:** Write and publish blog posts to help with SEO and educate your visitors.
- **FAQs:** Add common Questions and Answers to help customers understand your process before they apply.

### 🛡️ 9. Users (Staff) & Audit Logs
**What it is:** Your employee management and security cameras.
**What you do here:** 
- **Users:** Create accounts for your employees (like Loan Officers or Support Staff) so they can help you manage the system.
- **Audit Logs:** See exactly *who* did *what* and *when*. If an application was approved, the Audit Log will tell you which employee clicked the button.

---

### 💡 A Simple Daily Workflow Example:
1. **Morning:** You log in and check the **Dashboard** for new alerts.
2. **Review:** You go to **Applications** and see a new request from John Doe. You change his status to "Under Review."
3. **Verify:** You go to **Documents** to check John's uploaded PAN Card. It looks good, so you mark it "Verified".
4. **Approve:** You go back to his **Application** and change the status to "Approved". 

*Everything is connected to make your daily operations as smooth as possible!*
